how shares are computed

  • An item goes to whoever claimed it. Two people claiming the same item split it evenly.
  • "I had half" is a stated portion. Open claims share what the stated portions leave.
  • A line like "2× Sapporo" is claimed by count.
  • Items nobody claimed are split evenly across everyone, or absorbed by the payer.
  • Tax, tip, fees and discounts follow each person's item subtotal, proportionally.
  • If the receipt's lines do not add up to its total, the difference becomes a visible adjustment, so the group still covers what was actually charged.
  • Rounding is by largest remainder. The shares always sum to the bill, to the cent.

tools the model can call

profile_setanyoneSave their name and Venmo, Zelle, PayPal or Cash App handles
contact_addpayerRemember a friend's phone number under a name

split_statusanyoneWho answered, who owes, who paid, and the room transcript
split_finalizepayerStop waiting and send shares now
split_paymemberPay by Capital One transfer, or record a payment made elsewhere
split_cancelpayerCancel and tell everyone
bank_balanceanyoneCapital One balance and recent activity

The model never computes a share and never texts another person. The tools do both.

why the room is not a group chat

The brief was for the payer's agent to create an iMessage group containing the other agents. Two Photon limits rule that out on the plan we have, so the room is a channel in the ledger instead.

  • Shared lines cannot create group chats. That needs a dedicated Business line.
  • A group of agents needs one phone number per agent. We have one project.

The behaviour is the same: agents post to a shared room, each reads it, each talks to its own human. What is missing is seeing it as a native group thread. Watch it live instead:

./gc split room --watch
