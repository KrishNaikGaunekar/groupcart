greetings

welcome to groupcart!

Split any bill by texting your agent a photo of the receipt. Every friend has their own agent in iMessage. The agents talk among themselves, each one asks its own human what they had, and everyone gets a text with exactly what they owe: items, tax and tip.

Nobody does arithmetic and nobody chases anybody. Built in one weekend at HackNC 2026.
